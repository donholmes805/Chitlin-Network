Southern Soul Sessions

Soul Stage TV-MA Music / Documentary 2024

Journey into the heart of the modern South where the legacy of blues, gospel, and R&B converges. Southern Soul Sessions brings you front-row performances and intimate interviews with the architects of today's most vibrant cultural soundscape. From the backroads of Mississippi to the high-tech studios of Atlanta, witness the stories behind the songs that define a generation.
